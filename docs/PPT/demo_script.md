## Minute 0:30–1:30 — AI Triage (THE STAR FEATURE)

**SHOW:**
- Click **"AI Triage"** module icon
- Enter symptoms: "fever, body ache, difficulty breathing" (typed or voice)
- AI result card appears with triage level, probable conditions, and recommended action

**SAY:**
> "This is our star feature — AI-powered triage. Our ML model, trained on 50,000+ clinical records, predicts probable conditions in under 3 seconds. It works offline, in Marathi, Hindi, or English, and tells the ASHA worker whether the patient needs a PHC visit, telemedicine, or immediate emergency referral. No specialist needed for the first assessment."

**POINT TO:**
- Symptom input box (voice icon + text)
- Triage result: **"Moderate — Recommend CHC referral within 24 hours"**
- Confidence score bar
- "Recommended Actions" checklist
- Language toggle (Marathi / Hindi / English)

---

## Minute 1:30–2:30 — Emergency Escalation (THE SHOWSTOPPER)

**SHOW:**
- Click **"Emergency SOS"** module
- Dashboard switches to emergency view: "Patient: Ramesh Patil, Age 45, Chest Pain"
- See real-time status chain: ASHA Worker → PHC → CHC → 108 Ambulance → District Hospital
- Click **"Trigger SOS"** — see alert propagate across all connected panels

**SAY:**
> "Now, let's look at the most critical scenario — a patient with chest pain in a village 40 km from the nearest CHC. Watch what happens when our ASHA worker triggers the SOS. In seconds, the PHC in-charge, the CHC doctor, the 108 ambulance, and the district hospital ICU all receive synchronized alerts. This is how we stop the 40% referral drop-off dead in its tracks."

**POINT TO:**
- Emergency map view: patient location pin, ambulance ETA, nearest hospital bed
- Status chain indicators (green checkmarks as each node acknowledges)
- Notification timeline: "0:02 — PHC received | 0:05 — 108 dispatched | 0:08 — CHC confirmed"
- Patient vitals chart updating live

No more phone calls, no more lost registers. This data also feeds into the district health officer's analytics dashboard for better planning."

**POINT TO:**

This is ArogyaSetu+ — one platform, every facility, every patient, AI-powered. Thank you. We welcome your questions."
